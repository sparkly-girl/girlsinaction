What it is

Girls in AI is for members who are curious about artificial intelligence and technology. Together we learn how AI tools work, talk about how to use them responsibly, and use them to build solutions to problems we care about.

Featured in Girls in AI

Technovation in Fremont

Technovation is a global program where teams of young people find a real problem in their community and build a technology solution for it, with mentors along the way. Girls in AI supports Fremont-area girls forming Technovation teams, organized by season.
