What is Technovation?

Technovation is a global nonprofit program that helps young people learn to solve real problems with technology. Teams identify a challenge in their own community, design a solution, build a working prototype (such as a mobile app or an AI-powered tool), and present their idea to judges. Mentors support teams at every stage.

For official program details, rules and dates, see technovation.org (opens in a new tab).

How it fits in Girls in AI

Technovation is a natural fit for the Learn, Build, Share, Lead circle: girls learn AI and app tools, build a real solution, share it with judges and the community, and come back as mentors for the next season.

About the 2027 season

This season, Fremont-area girls will team up, pick a problem they care about in our community, and build a mobile app or AI-powered solution for it with help from mentors. At the end of the season, teams submit their project to the Technovation Challenge.

Who it's for

  • Girls in Fremont and nearby areas, especially middle schoolers
  • Beginners welcome: curiosity matters more than experience
  • Girls who care about a problem at school, at home or in our city
