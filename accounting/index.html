What it is

Girls in Accounting is about money skills for independence: budgeting, saving, understanding how money works and building confidence with numbers, for everyday life and for future careers.

What members do

The same Learn, Build, Share, Lead circle, applied to accounting.

  1. 1

    Learn

    Learn money basics like budgeting and saving with peers.

  2. 2

    Build

    Build something practical, such as a budget, tracker or small project plan.

  3. 3

    Share

    Share what you learned and help others with their money questions.

  4. 4

    Lead

    Lead a workshop or start a new money-skills group.
