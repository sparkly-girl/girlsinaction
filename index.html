Technovation in Fremont 2027

Learn and build with AI. This is where our Fremont Technovation teams live.

What is Technovation?

Technovation is a global nonprofit program where young people learn to solve real problems with technology. Teams find a challenge in their own community, design a solution, build a working prototype (like a mobile app or an AI-powered tool), and present their idea to judges.

Mentors support teams at every stage, so no one needs coding experience to start.

Official details at technovation.org (opens in a new tab)

Who it's for

  • Girls in Fremont and nearby areas, especially middle schoolers
  • Beginners welcome: curiosity matters more than experience
  • Girls who care about a problem at school, at home, or in our city
