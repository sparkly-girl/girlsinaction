How it works

Every member moves through the same circle then brings the next one in.

  1. 1

    Learn

    Pick a topic and learn it together with peers.

  2. 2

    Build

    Make something real: a project, tool, or artwork.

  3. 3

    Share

    Show your work and teach what you learned.

  4. 4

    Lead

    Guide the next group and start new chapters.

Then every leader helps the next learner, and the circle grows.
